What it does

🚗 Live-traffic leave times

Leave By looks at where you're going next and checks real traffic to tell you when to leave.

📅 Google Calendar sync

Optionally connect Google Calendar (read-only) to bring in classes, work shifts like WhenToWork, and other plans.

🚌 Car vs. transit

Compare driving with buses and trains for the same trip, using real schedules.

🗓️ Day, week & month views

See every drive and stop for the day, or spot your busy days at a glance.

📸 Screenshot import

Snap your class schedule and Leave By reads it for you, so there's less typing.

🔒 Private by design

Sign in with Google. Your schedule is saved to your account and only you can see it.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google.
  2. Add your places, like home, campus, and work, with their addresses.
  3. Add your schedule by hand, from a screenshot, or by connecting Google Calendar.
  4. Open Leave By each morning to see when to leave, with live traffic.

How Leave By uses your Google data

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Leave By requests read-only access. It reads events only from the calendars you pick, and uses each event's title, time, and location to plan your route and leave times. It never creates, changes, or deletes calendar events, and it never sells or shares your data. Leave By's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in our privacy policy.
